01 — Manifesto

Two humans One GPU Zero templates

A screen is a stage. Pixels are performers. Motion is the script.

NOVA is a small motion lab. We prototype in shaders, mix in the browser, and ship sites that hum. Every curve is tuned by hand, every transition has a reason, every frame is paid for in milliseconds.

No stock sections. No borrowed easing. If it does not move like it weighs something, it does not leave the room.
